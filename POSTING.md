private, which the bounty refuses).

`ad.mp4` in this repository (also at
https://github.com/alibima2002/bring-the-humans-03/raw/master/ad.mp4):
1080×1920 vertical, 17.5 seconds, H.264 + AAC. Download it to your phone as it
is. Do NOT edit, trim, add effects, filters or text to it in TikTok's editor or
anywhere else — post the file exactly as rendered.

## Before you post

- Post from a **public** TikTok account (Settings and privacy → Privacy →
  Private account **off**).
- The video must be posted **before Wed 25 Nov 02:00 WIB** (deadline
  2026-11-24T19:00:00Z) and after the opening (Fri 21 Nov 19:00 UTC — already
  passed, so any time now until the deadline is fine).
- Keep the video up, public and viewable in the US, and the account public,

## How to post (TikTok app)

1. Open TikTok, tap **+**, pick `ad.mp4` from your gallery.
2. On the posting screen set **Who can watch this video: Everyone**.
3. Paste this caption EXACTLY (copy-paste, do not retype the code):

```
Your AI agent can win real money 🤖💰
Made by my AI agent for a $500 bounty on make.fun, where AI agents compete for real money: make.fun/bounty/bring-the-humans-03
makefun-clarinet-leaf-castle
Join with my code 8q4hj8xe: https://make.fun/r/8q4hj8xe
```

   - The second line is required word for word: it carries `$500 bounty` and
     `make.fun`.
   - `makefun-clarinet-leaf-castle` is the play code for this bounty (code 0 of
     Ali's 4). It must appear in the caption exactly as written (or in the
     account bio instead — caption is simpler).
   - The last line is Ali's referral link (optional for the gate, but it is
     what the ad is for).
4. Post it as a **video** (not a photo post).
5. Do NOT pay to promote it (no TikTok Promote, no view-buying, no bots, no
   loops): bought, botted or farmed views disqualify the entry. Sharing the
   video and asking real people to watch it is fine.

## After you post — send the link back

1. Open the video on **tiktok.com in a browser** (not the app's Share → Copy
   link, which gives a short `vm.tiktok.com` link that the gate refuses).
2. Copy the full address it lands on. It must look like this:

```
https://www.tiktok.com/@<your-handle>/video/<digits-only-id>
```

3. Send that exact address back. The agent then puts it in `VIDEO.json`,
   re-runs the checks, and submits — only with your explicit approval.

## If the caption needs fixing later

TikTok lets you edit a caption (video → three-dot More → Edit post, where it
offers it). Fix the caption there and tell the agent; the same commit can be
submitted again. If TikTok offers no Edit post, the video has to be posted
again with the fixed caption and `VIDEO.json` updated to the new video.
